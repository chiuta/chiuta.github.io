# Spaced repetition in university teaching

Spaced repetition schedules each review shortly before the learner is expected to forget the item. In a semester-long study with 212 students, groups that used spaced review scored 14 percent higher on the final exam than groups that crammed the week before.

The effect was strongest for factual material such as definitions and dates. For open-ended problem solving the gain was small and not statistically significant.

Students reported that the daily review sessions, about ten minutes long, were easier to keep up than long weekly sessions.

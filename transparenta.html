What was checked

  • Audit 1 (2026-10-21): 110 apps. Audit 2 (2026-10-22): 109 apps measured again, with a different and stricter method. The multi-file repository Pendulul is a book, not a web app, and is excluded from audit 2.
  • Chromium headless (Playwright), desktop 1366×850 and mobile 360×740 with touch, ro-RO locale.
  • Security: hostile text in every field (580 injections in 301 fields in audit 1), hostile file uploads (JSON with __proto__, CSV with formulas, random binary, 5 MB text), payloads in URL and hash, about 2001 random clicks, a run with the CSP removed to detect latent execution.
  • Accessibility: axe-core (WCAG 2.0/2.1/2.2 A and AA), keyboard traversal and focus visibility, accessible names, exactly one H1, zoom not blocked, touch targets of at least 24 px, text of at least 12 px, horizontal overflow at 360 px.
  • Offline: every app reopened after a first visit with the network cut; presence and behaviour of the service worker.
  • Honesty of the technical label (fff-info): service worker, hosts, languages, size; catalog versions compared with the apps.
  • Storage: localStorage keys without the app prefix, collisions on a shared origin, secrets kept in clear text.

Not evaluated: correctness of content (game rules, astronomical data, quality of translations), Safari and iOS, real devices. 3D apps ran with software rendering, so their timings are pessimistic. The per-app sheets of audit 1 contained simulated user reviews; they are not reproduced here.
